Swizo: Ball Rash — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Swizo: Ball Rash ("Swizo", "the Game") is provided by Arnon Tsarfati ("we", "us"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Game.

Information you provide

You do not need to register an account or provide your name, email address, or other contact information to play Swizo. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond to your inquiry.

Gameplay data stored on your device

The Game may store gameplay information, such as scores, progress, and sound or gameplay preferences, locally on your device to provide its features. This information is not necessarily removed merely by closing the Game. You can manage app data using your device settings. Depending on your device's settings and platform backup behavior, some app data may be backed up by your operating system.

Third-party services and platform data

Swizo is distributed through Google Play. Google may independently process information related to downloads, installation, security, and use of Google Play services under its own privacy terms. The Android operating system may also process device and diagnostic information. This policy does not claim that Google or your device provider collects no information.

If we add advertising, analytics, crash reporting, online accounts, or other services that collect information through the Game, we will update this policy and any required store disclosures before enabling them.

How information is used

Locally stored gameplay information supports game functions and preferences. Information you voluntarily send by email is used to respond to you and handle related requests.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored game information may remain until you clear the Game's data or uninstall it, subject to platform backup and restoration behavior. Emails you send us may be retained as reasonably needed to respond, maintain records, and comply with applicable obligations. You may request deletion of information you have sent us by contacting the address below, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Children's privacy

Swizo does not require users to provide personal contact details to play. Parents or guardians with privacy questions about a child's use of the Game may contact us. We will handle such requests in accordance with applicable law.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes

We may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the Game or applicable requirements. The updated policy will be posted at this page with a revised effective date.
